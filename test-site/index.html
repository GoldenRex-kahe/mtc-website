ABOUT MTC

🌟 ABOUT THIS COMMUNITY

MC Thamizh Creators is a dedicated space built exclusively for Tamil Minecraft YouTubers who aim to grow, learn, and succeed together. This community brings every creator, big or small, into one safe and friendly environment where ideas, creativity, and collaborations 🤝 can freely develop.

Here, you can discuss content plans, share video concepts, get inspiration, and collaborate with other Tamil creators who share the same passion for Minecraft. Whether you're an experienced YouTuber or just starting your journey, this server helps you build confidence, improve your style, and stay motivated.

Our focus is to create a united Tamil Minecraft community that supports each other and grows together.

🎬 EDITING

We offer dedicated editing channels where creators can learn editing techniques, share templates, request help, and improve overall video quality. This space encourages skill-sharing, feedback, and resource exchange so every creator can level up their editing game.

🎉 EVENTS

We host fun and engaging Minecraft events for all members of the community. Some events may require forms or registrations, and all updates are announced in the server. These events help creators bond, participate actively, and enjoy the community experience.

🎯 GOAL

To unite Tamil Minecraft YouTubers, encourage collaboration, support growth, and build a fun, strong, and creative Tamil Minecraft community.

HOW TO TAKE PART

  1. Apply to joinSend your YouTube channel on our application page. Once an admin approves it, you get your own Discord invite.
  2. Get the Creator roleAsk an admin. It is for people who make Minecraft videos.
  3. Log in here with DiscordAdd your YouTube channel on your account. Once an admin verifies it you appear in Creators.
  4. Register for the next eventOpen the event and press Register. Admins confirm places.
  5. Share your videoAfter the event, add your screenshots or YouTube video to the showcase.

EVENT RULES

Each event has its own rules on its event page. These apply to every event:

  • Respect every player, in game and in Discord.
  • No hacked clients, x-ray, or exploits unless an event says so.
  • Admin decisions on event days are final. Raise problems in Discord afterwards.
  • If you record, credit MTC and the event in your video.

OUR DATA PROMISE

Our December 2025 event was lost to a hosting failure. Since then, all event records are saved every day, and screenshots and videos are kept separate from the game server.
